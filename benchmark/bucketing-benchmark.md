# Bucketed `binary_kernel!` timing

Wall-clock of the multithreaded bucketed kernel on operators time-evolved
under several models, vs the serial baseline. Generated by
`benchmark/bucketing/run_sweep.jl`. See `bucketing-loadbalance.md` for the
per-strategy load-balance analysis.

- Thread counts: 1, 2, 4, 8
- Strategies: Serial, Folded{8}, Mixing{8}
- `commutator_HO` = `[H, O(t)]` (the RK4 kernel call); `sqr_OO` = `O(t)·O(t)`.

## Notes

- `Serial` is the reference single-threaded kernel — identical to the `main` branch.
- **vs main @t=1** isolates the bucketing win on a single thread (smaller, cache-
  resident per-bucket dictionaries instead of one large one); **thread scaling** is
  the multithreading speedup of a strategy over its own single-thread time; **best
  vs main** is the combined wall-clock speedup over `main`.
- Operands have one small operand (`H`) for `commutator_HO`; `sqr_OO` is the
  heavier `O·O` workload that benefits most from threads.
